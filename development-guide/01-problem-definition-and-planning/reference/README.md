# Part 1 practical reference

**Status:** 0.5.0-draft.2 — draft

**Purpose:** Help a builder connect the four Part 1 planning records.

**Use with:** The [Part 1 overview](../README.md), the chapter you are working in, the [glossary pointers](../../../glossary.md#part-1-planning-foundations), and the [requirement index](../../../requirement-index.md#part-1--problem-definition-and-planning).

This page owns no requirement IDs; where it or a template is shorter than a chapter's `## Requirements`, the chapter controls.

## Build the planning record in order

1. **Establish the user need and current work.** Use [1.1 Defining the Problem](../01-defining-the-problem.md) and the [problem brief](../../../templates/1.1/problem-brief.yaml). Carry forward the bounded task, evidence status, assumptions, scope, dependencies and downstream consumer.
2. **State what a worthwhile result means.** Use [1.2 Requirements, Success Criteria, and Measurable Targets](../02-requirements-success-criteria-and-measurable-targets.md) and the [success-criteria record](../../../templates/1.2/success-criteria.yaml). Carry forward the outcome, acceptance logic, separate decision dimensions, baseline status and evidence handoff.
3. **Compare the smallest plausible approaches.** Use [1.3 Agent Suitability and Alternatives](../03-agent-suitability-and-alternatives.md) and the [suitability decision](../../../templates/1.3/suitability-decision.yaml). Carry forward the shared task cases, simpler baseline, rationale, uncertainty and any unproven added-value claim.
4. **Screen early conditions and route open work.** Use [1.4 Initial Risk, Data, Ownership, and Delivery Plan](../04-initial-risk-data-ownership-and-delivery-plan.md) and the [initial scope record](../../../templates/1.4/initial-scope-record.yaml). Carry forward harm and affected-person questions, source, access and capability facts, owners, applicability, containment and restart questions, and specialist handoffs.

The four templates share one fictional planning record; treat its values as structure, not observations, results or approvals.

## Practical use and review

1. **Keep evidence status visible.** Do not promote a reported difficulty, an assumption or a planned target into an observed result when copying fields between records.
2. **Keep the comparison fair.** Carry the same task, source and access conditions, and outcome claim into the suitability decision; a fixed workflow can stay preferred.
3. **Make unknowns a handoff, not a pass.** When risk, data handling, authority, evidence, rollout or execution detail is unresolved, name the owner and next Part in the scope record.
4. **Use later Parts for proof and authorization.** [Part 4](../../04-testing-and-evaluation/README.md) designs evidence and [Part 5](../../05-release-operations-and-lifecycle/README.md) covers release and operations. A completed template is not deployment authorization.

Use [applicability](../../../applicability.md) to decide which capability, security, execution or specialized-assurance work applies.
