## Applicability guide

- Assess the **AUTH** and **BOUNDARY** groups for each production candidate. Record a visible no-route or absent-trigger rationale rather than creating an implicit route.
- Assess the **DATA** group when the candidate processes non-public or personal data. Assess `DATA-05` when a consequential event is proposed. Keep the DATA group unresolved rather than marking it not applicable when classification or a copy path is unknown.
- Record `met`, `not_applicable`, `unmet`, or `exception_proposed` for each individual ID. A group name is a navigation aid, not a substitute for an individual applicability record.
- Consult the canonical [applicability guide](https://github.com/X-McKay/playbooks/blob/151fca01fbc609c5550c563c902946db540c2f4c/applicability.md) for trigger, exception, readiness, and non-waivable-gate interpretation.
